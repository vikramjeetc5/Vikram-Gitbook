### Workplace Policies

<details>

<summary>Code of conduct</summary>

* Treat colleagues, customers, partners, and visitors with respect, in person and online.
* Communicate professionally in email, chat, and meetings.
* Do not engage in harassment, discrimination, bullying, or intimidation.
* Report serious concerns through the proper channel. The company aims to let you raise concerns without fear of retaliation.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Attendance, leave, and working hours</summary>

* Follow the working schedule from your manager or HR, and record attendance accurately.
* Request leave through the approved process, and tell your manager about unexpected absences.
* Attend required meetings, training sessions, and onboarding activities.
* For entitlements and approval timelines, see the HR policies or contact the HR team.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Remote work</summary>

* Remote work needs company approval.
* Use authorized devices and connect through the company VPN (see **How to Configure VPN**).
* Keep devices and documents secure, and avoid shared or public devices.
* Report a lost device or suspected compromise immediately.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Email and internet use</summary>

* Use official company channels for business communication.
* Verify unexpected requests for sensitive information or urgent payments.
* Do not click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments, and report phishing attempts.
* Do not send confidential information to unauthorized recipients or to personal email accounts.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Data protection and confidentiality</summary>

* Confidential information includes customer and employee records, business plans, financial information, source code, credentials, API keys, and contracts.
* Access only what your role needs, store and share files through approved company systems, and check recipients before sending.
* Do not keep company data in personal accounts, devices, or cloud services.
* Your duty to protect this information continues after you leave the company.
* Report suspected data loss or accidental disclosure promptly.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Privacy and monitoring</summary>

* Company systems, devices, and accounts may be monitored or audited where the law and company policy allow, for security, compliance, and troubleshooting.
* See the privacy notice or contact HR for details about the information collected and your rights.

</details>

### AI and External Tools

{% hint style="danger" %}
Unless it is expressly approved, do not enter confidential company data, customer information, personal data, credentials, or source code into public AI tools or unauthorized websites.
{% endhint %}

Use only company-approved AI tools for work involving company information, and review AI-generated content before you rely on it.

### Reporting a Security Incident

{% hint style="warning" %}
Report straight away if you notice a lost or stolen device, a suspicious email or link, a suspected account compromise, unauthorized access, accidental sharing of confidential information, a malware alert, or an unexpected MFA request.
{% endhint %}

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Stop the activity

If it is safe to do so, stop what may be causing the problem.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Leave things as they are

Do not delete evidence, investigate on your own, or try unapproved fixes.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Contact the right team

Reach IT Support or the security team through the approved channel (see the contacts below).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Describe what happened

Say what happened, when it happened, and include any error messages. Then follow the team's instructions.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Report even if it was your mistake.** Report incidents promptly and honestly, even if you think a mistake of your own caused them.
{% endhint %}

### Policy Violations

Failing to follow company policies may lead to corrective or disciplinary action, in line with the law, employment agreements, and company procedures. Depending on the case, the company may restrict system access, require training, or investigate. If you have a concern, raise it early through the proper channel.
