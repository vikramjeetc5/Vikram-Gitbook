Welcome to your first day at VC Technologies! This page is your checklist for getting set up. Each step links to a page with the full details, so you can follow the order here and open a guide only when you need it.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Goal:** a secure, working setup before you start your assigned work.
{% endhint %}

### Before You Start

You should have your company laptop or desktop, your company email address with activation instructions, and your initial login details. If anything is missing, contact IT Support.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Never share your password or verification codes with anyone. If you have not received your login details, contact IT Support instead of creating an account yourself.
{% endhint %}

### Setup Steps

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Set up your workstation

Connect your laptop to power and the approved company Wi-Fi, and check that it starts normally. Tell IT Support about any missing or faulty equipment.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Sign in and secure your account

Sign in with your company credentials, change the temporary password, and set up multifactor authentication (MFA). Your password must meet the **Password Policy**. If you get locked out, see **How to Reset Your Password**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Set up your email

Open the company email app or webmail, sign in, and send a test message to check that it works.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Install required software

Open **Tools and Software** for the applications your role needs and how to install them.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Get access and connect

Follow **How to Request Access** for any system you cannot open. If you will work remotely, follow **How to Configure VPN**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Check your tools and security

Confirm that your messaging and meeting apps work, and that your device follows the **Device Control Guidelines** (updates on, screen lock on).
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### Final Check

{% hint style="success" %}
Your setup is complete when you can sign in to your device, use your company email, open your required software, reach the systems you need, and use your messaging and meeting tools. If anything is still pending, tell your manager and IT Support.
{% endhint %}
