# Welcome

## Welcome to VC Technologies

We are delighted to have you on board. This documentation portal is designed to help you get familiar with our organization, IT environment, tools, processes, and workplace policies.

Whether you are joining us for the first time or need to refer back to internal guidelines, this portal will serve as your central resource for essential employee and IT information.

### Getting Started

Your first day is the beginning of your journey with us. To help you settle in smoothly, we have created a dedicated **Getting Started** folder containing the essential information you need to begin your work.

Inside this folder, you will find:

### How to Use This Documentation Portal

This portal is organized into three sections to help you find the information you need quickly.

* **Getting Started:** Essential resources for new employees, including first-day setup, tools and software, and company policies.
* **How-to Guides:** Step-by-step instructions for common IT and workplace tasks, with troubleshooting tips.
* **IT Policies and Standards:** Security requirements, acceptable use guidelines, and the IT standards that apply across the organization.

### Need Help?

If you encounter any issues with account access, workstation setup, software installation, or other IT-related tasks, please contact the **IT Support team**. For questions related to onboarding, HR documentation, or employee policies, please reach out to the **HR team**.
